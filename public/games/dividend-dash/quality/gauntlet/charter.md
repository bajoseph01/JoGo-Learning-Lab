# Dividend Dash Polish Charter

## Learner and objective

Grade 4-6 learner practising the long-division sequence and producing paper-transferable working.

## Fantasy, verbs, and loop

- Fantasy: build a safe number route for Mr Jo.
- Verbs: read, steer, choose, lock, inspect, repair.
- Loop: read current working number → choose a valid number-gate → place it into the tableau → receive mathematical feedback → continue or repair.

## Device and input contract

- Weakest required viewport: 1024×768 landscape.
- Additional supported viewport: 820×1180 portrait.
- Inputs: Pointer Events/touch, mouse, keyboard.
- Session: 3-8 minutes.

## Non-negotiables

- Academic steps cannot be bypassed.
- The original prototypes remain preserved.
- DEV sessions never save.
- JoGo brand and character boards are the visual source of truth.
- Accuracy precedes speed.
