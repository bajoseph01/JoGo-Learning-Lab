- `../../../jogo-game-builder/assets/authoritative/jogo-character-board.png`

The supplied JoGo brand board and character sheet lead. The game adapts their visual system to a racing-route fantasy without inventing a new identity.

## Palette

- JoGo Red `#D91515`: primary action and urgent route state.
- Power Blue `#1E3ABA`: brand structure, track, and focus.
- Lightning Yellow `#F7C948`: gates, progress, and energetic highlights.
- Chalk White `#F8FCFB`: surfaces and primary text on dark blue.
- Charcoal `#2B2F38`: copy and deep neutral.
- Success Green `#2FAE66`: verified route sections and positive feedback.

No learner-facing palette toggle: the approved brand board is authoritative and consistency is part of the fidelity check.

## Typography roles

- DM Sans variable, bundled locally.
- Display: 800-900 weight, compact line-height.
- Prompts and answers: at least 22px on desktop/iPad.
- Labels: at least 15px, uppercase with generous tracking.

## Button and material language

- rounded tactile controls;
- clear blue/red/yellow semantic roles;
- 3D press states using a short lower edge and translate-on-press;
- white cards with soft blue shadows over a deep-blue game field.

## Hero assets

- Mr Jo skating action pose is the player marker.
- Robot sidekick supports start and success moments.
- The long-division tableau is the academic hero object and remains visually dominant.

## Motion language

Fast but readable: lane movement, gate travel, route confirmation, and short feedback pulses. Reduced-motion mode removes non-essential travel and bounce.

## Do not

- replace the supplied JoGo identity with generic neon racing UI;
- use emojis as primary artwork;
- allow decorative lane marks to resemble mathematical notation;
- cover the tableau with persistent HUD panels;
- communicate correctness through colour alone.
