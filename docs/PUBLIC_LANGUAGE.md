# Public language guide

The public page should sound like a teacher explaining a purposeful lesson resource to another adult.

## Lead with

- the curriculum concept;
- what learners physically or mentally do;
- how feedback helps them correct an idea;
- the length and place of the activity in a lesson;
- evidence a teacher can observe.

## Preferred terms

- interactive learning experience
- activity
- focused practice
- learning challenge
- concept
- learner action
- feedback
- evidence of learning
- teacher-selected

## Avoid in public copy

- game hub
- arcade
- gaming portal
- free play
- earn coins to learn
- fun instead of work
- screen-time reward
- edutainment

The activity itself may have a playful title. The surrounding explanation must make its academic purpose plain.

## Tone sample

> Learners build exact South African money amounts with coins and notes, then find another combination with the same value. The activity makes equivalence visible and gives immediate feedback when an amount is short or too large.

Do not make broad claims such as “guaranteed mastery” or “curriculum approved” without evidence.
